# Logic foundations

> [!warning] Fictional practice
> These are Primer-authored examples. They are not UCSC past-paper questions and carry no official marks.

## Implication

1. Write the converse and contrapositive of: "If an integer is divisible by 6, then it is divisible by 3."
2. Give a valuation where $p \to q$ is true but its converse is false.

## Equivalence

1. Use a truth table to compare $p \to q$ and $\neg q \to \neg p$.
2. Explain in one sentence why matching final columns establish logical equivalence.
