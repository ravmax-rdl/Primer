Research `$ARGUMENTS` into one evidence table before drafting prose.

Use `find.md`, `source.md`, `cite.md`, `synth.md`, `summary.md`, `gap.md`, and
`paper-review.md` from the academic-workflow internal references as needed.

1. Resolve the research question, scope, and one target note.
2. Search vault and Zotero sources before the web. Resolve PDF status before
   claiming page evidence.
3. Build rows for claim, evidence, source, page or section, strength,
   counterevidence, and acceptance decision.
4. Separate direct support, derivation, external corroboration, inference, and
   unverified claims. Never cite an unreadable source as support.
5. Deduplicate sources by stable identifier where available. Preserve conflicting
   evidence instead of averaging it away.
6. Draft prose only from accepted rows. Mark unresolved gaps explicitly.

Write only the target note. Do not update learner mastery or cards. End with
accepted claims, rejected or unresolved evidence, and the next research action.
